# Ledger Mortis — Product
Pitch: Paste your transactions. Summon the ghosts of your money. Banish the ones haunting you.
Audience: anyone who wants to see where their money goes; global judges and users, no prior knowledge of M-Pesa.
Flow: Summon (import) -> Seance (graveyard reveal) -> Exorcism (banish recurring charges, see savings).
Principles: no login, no backend, demo data one click away, every spooky element must carry data or function.
Out of scope: accounts, real payments, bank APIs, cloud storage.
